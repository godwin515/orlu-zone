Orlu Zone at a glance

Orlu Zone is the Imo West Senatorial District of Imo State, in south-eastern Nigeria. It is the largest of the state's three senatorial zones, alongside Owerri and Okigwe.

    The Orashi rises from a spring at Dikenafai, in the Ideato area, and flows past Oguta Lake on its way to Rivers State and the Niger Delta.

Aim and objectives

Our aim

To create an authoritative, prestigious historical record and living legacy that documents, celebrates, and connects the distinguished sons and daughters of Orlu Zone at home and in the diaspora, while promoting Aku Ruo Ulo: home investment, unity and sustainable development of Orlu Land.

Our objectives

  • Honour

    To identify, document and honour distinguished individuals, families and institutions whose service, enterprise, leadership and philanthropy have contributed to the growth of Orlu Zone.

  • Preserve

    To preserve and promote the rich cultural heritage, history, values and traditions of Orlu people for present and future generations.

  • Invest

    To showcase viable home and diaspora investments, business opportunities and enterprise innovations that encourage Aku Ruo Ulo Na Orlu Zone for Ndi Orlu.

  • Unite

    To foster unity, collaboration and development synergy among Orlu people at home and in the diaspora (USA, Germany and worldwide) under the spirit of Nke a bu nke anyi (This is Our Own).

  • Connect

    To serve as a credible reference compendium, networking platform and legacy archive for governance, investment, mentorship, youth inspiration and socio-economic advancement of Orlu Zone.

  • Recognise

    To institutionalise a transparent, merit-based recognition system that inspires excellence, integrity and community service.
