About Orlu Zone

Orlu Zone is the Imo West Senatorial District of Imo State, in south-eastern Nigeria. It is the largest of the state's three senatorial zones and is made up of 12 local government areas. Orlu town is widely regarded as the commercial hub of Imo State.

The Orashi River

The Orashi River (also called Urashi or Ulasi) has its source in Imo State and is one of the state's significant rivers. It flows past Oguta Lake, in the Oguta area of Orlu Zone, on its way to Rivers State and the Niger Delta.

Waterway

The Federal Government approved dredging the river from Oguta Lake to Degema to improve inland waterways.

  1. Source

    The river rises from a spring at Dikenafai, in the Ideato area of Imo State.

  2. Oguta Lake

    The Orashi flows past the south-western part of Oguta Lake. The Njaba River is the lake's major inflow.

  3. To the coast

    The river route runs through Oguta, Ndoni, Abonnema and Degema, in Rivers State, to the coast.

Aku Ruo Ulo: invest at home

Siri na mba, no ulo na Orlu Zone.

Bring wealth home: invest in a home or real estate in Orlu Zone.

Overview

Investing in real estate or building a home in Orlu Zone (Imo West Senatorial District), Imo State, is a strategic move. It is driven by strong diaspora interest, major infrastructure expansion, and the zone's standing as a commercial and academic hub. The region spans twelve local government areas with thriving commercial and industrial centres.

Aku Ruo Ulo Siri Na Mba: it will help in creating jobs and wealth.

Core drivers for home investment

  • Infrastructure and urban renewal

    Federal and state governments are concluding dualization and reconstruction projects, including the Orlu–Mgbee–Akokwa–Uga Road, the Anara–Nwangele–Nkwerre–Orlu Road and the Orlu–Awo Idemili–Ihiala Road. The dualization of the Mgbidi–Omuma–Okporo Road has unlocked commercial activity. The Government House Annex in Eziachi, Orlu, brings government closer to the people and lifts nearby property values.

  • Strong anchor institutions

    Property near the zone's institutions enjoys high rental demand and steady appreciation:

    • Imo State University Teaching Hospital (IMSUTH), Orlu town
    • Imo State School of Nursing and Health Technology, Okporo
    • Imo International Market, one of West Africa's most influential pharmaceutical and commercial hubs

  • The diaspora “Aku Ruo Ulo” influx

    Orlu is home to some of Nigeria's most successful industrialists and diaspora members. There is steady demand for premium residential homes, gated estates and country homes, especially during festive periods.

Smart investment paths

  • Residential rental yields

    Building or buying blocks of flats and mini-estates near Okporo, Eziachi or Orlu town targets medical students, doctors, civil servants and business merchants.

  • Gated estate land banking

    Buying plots in planned private layouts, such as Arugo Gardens Estate, or on developing suburban borders offers a lower entry cost and helps shield an investment from local land disputes.

  • Commercial real estate

    Land facing major connecting roads, such as the Orlu–Owerri or Mgbidi–Okporo expressways, offers long-term value for plazas, hospitality and corporate offices.
